#### Bio


I am a Postdoctoral Fellow jointly affiliated with [Tsinghua University](https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/csen/) and [Zhipu AI](https://www.zhipuai.cn/zh), fortunately working with Prof. Jie Tang.

Previously, I received my Ph.D. from the Knowledge Engineering Group (KEG), Department of Computer Science and Technology, [Tsinghua University](https://www.cs.tsinghua.edu.cn/csen/) in 2025, advised by Prof. Bin Xu and Prof. Juanzi Li.

I was fortunate to be a visiting student at the School of Computing, [National University of Singapore](https://www.comp.nus.edu.sg/), advised by Prof. Tat-Seng Chua.

Currently, I work on the foundations and development of large multimodal models (LMMs).
